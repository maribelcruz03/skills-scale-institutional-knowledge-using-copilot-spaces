# OctoAcme Project Management Docs

This directory contains OctoAcme's repeatable project management processes, roles, and guidance for delivering product features, services, and integrations. Use this README as the starting point for finding the process documentation and understanding how the documents fit together.

## Project Management Processes Summary

OctoAcme uses a customer-first, iterative, and data-informed approach with clear ownership. The lifecycle begins with **initiation**, where the business need, stakeholders, success metrics, risks, and resources are identified and a decision is made about whether to proceed to planning.

Approved work moves into **planning**, where the team creates a prioritized backlog, estimates scope, defines the Definition of Done, identifies dependencies and risks, and establishes milestones and a release plan. During **execution and tracking**, the team manages work on a project board, uses small pull requests, validates quality through automated and manual testing, monitors delivery and product signals, and escalates blockers through defined levels.

**Risk management and communication** continue throughout the lifecycle. Teams maintain a risk register, review risks and dependencies regularly, and provide consistent status updates to stakeholders. **Release and deployment** follow pre-release checks, CI and security validation, staging smoke tests, post-deployment verification, stakeholder communication, and rollback or incident procedures when needed.

After each sprint, release, or significant milestone, **retrospectives and continuous improvement** capture learnings and turn them into a small number of prioritized actions with owners and due dates. The **roles and personas** guidance clarifies responsibilities for project managers, product managers, developers, QA/testing, and stakeholders. Together, these practices create a transparent, repeatable lifecycle from idea through delivery and learning.
